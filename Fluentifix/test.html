Composition Topic: "My Daily Routine"

Write a short composition (5-7 sentences) about your daily routine.

Use the following questions to help you:

1. What time do you wake up?

2. What do you do in the morning?

3. What time do you go to school/work?

4. What do you do in the afternoon?

5. What do you do in the evening before bed?

Try to use simple sentences and basic connectors like "and," "then," and "after that."

Topic: My Favorite Place

Write a short composition (80-100 words) about your favorite place. Describe where it is, what it looks like, and why you like it. Use simple sentences and adjectives to give details.

You can include:

1. The name of the place

2. What you can do there

3. Who you go with

4. Why it is special to you

Example start:

"My favorite place is the park near my house. It has green trees, a small lake, and a playground. I go there with my friends on weekends..."

Topic: The Advantages and Disadvantages of Online Learning

Write a composition of 120-150 words discussing the advantages and disadvantages of online learning. In your composition, you should:

Describe at least two advantages of online learning.

Mention at least two disadvantages of online learning.

Give your own opinion on whether online learning is better than traditional learning.

Make sure to use clear and well-structured sentences. Use linking words such as however, on the other hand, in addition, and for example to make your writing more fluent.

Topic: The Impact of Technology on Daily Life

Enunciation:

In today's world, technology plays a crucial role in our everyday lives. From communication and work to entertainment and education, digital advancements have changed the way we live. However, some people believe that we rely too much on technology, which may have negative effects on our social interactions and well-being.

Write a composition of 180-220 words discussing the role of technology in daily life.

Include the following points:

Advantages of technology in everyday activities (e.g., convenience, access to information, efficiency).

Disadvantages of depending too much on technology (e.g., reduced face-to-face communication, health issues).

Your personal opinion: Do you think technology has improved our lives more than it has harmed them? Why or why not?

Use a clear structure with an introduction, body paragraphs, and a conclusion. Try to include linking words (e.g., however, therefore, on the other hand) and a variety of grammatical structures to show your B2-level skills.

Topic: The Role of Failure in Success

Enunciation:

Many people view failure as something negative, but others argue that it is an essential part of success. Throughout history, some of the most successful individuals have experienced failure before achieving their goals. However, some believe that failure can be discouraging and prevent people from trying again.

Write a 250-300 word essay discussing the importance of failure in achieving success.

Include the following points:

Arguments in favor of failure being a valuable learning experience (e.g., resilience, innovation, self-improvement).

Counterarguments: Can failure have negative effects, such as lowering motivation or causing financial loss?

Your personal opinion: Do you believe failure is necessary for success? Why or why not?

Use a formal tone, cohesive structure, and advanced grammatical structures (e.g., inversion, cleft sentences, participle clauses). Make sure to include linking words (nevertheless, consequently, on the contrary, not only…but also).

Topic: Is True Freedom an Illusion in Modern Society?

Enunciation:

Freedom is often regarded as one of the most fundamental human rights, yet in today's highly structured societies, some argue that true freedom is an illusion. Laws, social norms, economic pressures, and even digital surveillance shape and limit people's choices, often without them realizing it. On the other hand, many believe that these structures exist to maintain order and protect individuals, rather than restrict their freedoms.

Write a 300-350 word essay discussing whether true freedom exists in modern society.

Include the following points:

The meaning of freedom - Is it simply the absence of restrictions, or does it also involve responsibility and ethical considerations?

Factors that limit freedom, such as laws, societal expectations, and technology. Are these limitations necessary?

Counterarguments - Can freedom exist within boundaries? Do we enjoy more freedom today than in the past?

Your personal perspective - Do you believe we are truly free, or are we controlled in ways we fail to notice?

Use a formal and sophisticated tone, integrating advanced grammatical structures such as inversion, cleft sentences, participle clauses, and nominalization. Ensure cohesion through the use of discourse markers (moreover, conversely, notwithstanding, it could be argued that...).
