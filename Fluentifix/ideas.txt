Key Definitions for Your Website
✅ Fluency (Cambridge English, 2020)

The ability to speak, write, and understand a language smoothly and naturally without hesitation.

✅ Reading Comprehension (British Council, 2020)

The ability to process text, understand its meaning, and integrate it with prior knowledge. Strong reading skills improve vocabulary, grammar, and overall communication.

✅ Language Acquisition (Krashen, 2004)

The process of learning a language naturally through exposure and interaction, rather than memorization and conscious study.

✅ Active Learning (Harvard University, 2022)

An approach to learning where students engage with content through problem-solving, discussions, and self-assessment, leading to better retention of knowledge.

✅ Online Tutoring (OECD, 2020)

A virtual learning method that connects students with tutors via digital platforms, offering flexible, personalized education that improves engagement and accessibility.
